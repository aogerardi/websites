I want to list my stock for sale

Tell us how many shares you hold and what you are asking. We will post the listing and put interested shareholders in touch with you.

Submitting a listing request does not obligate you to sell. An FRC representative will confirm the details with you before anything is posted.

Email me when new shares are listed

Shares come available irregularly, often through estates. Join the notification list and we will email you when a new listing is posted.

We only use this address for new-listing notices. You can ask to be removed at any time.
