For Operators

Lease our minerals

FRC owns 104,000 net mineral acres across Oklahoma and neighbouring states. If you are developing a unit that includes our acreage, start here.

How we lease

We review every lease request against our title, the spacing and pooling picture, and the development plan for the unit. We are a long-term mineral owner, not a flipper — we would rather see a well drilled and produced properly than take the highest bonus on a lease that never gets developed.

Lease bonus income has been a meaningful part of our revenue, running between $185,000 and $1.45 million a year over the last decade.

What to send us: the Section-Township-Range, county and state, the proposed unit, your proposed terms (bonus, royalty, primary term) and your development timeline.

Where we own

Oklahoma is roughly 82% of our position at 85,583 net mineral acres, followed by Texas (6,189), Arkansas (5,700), Colorado (2,276), New Mexico (1,422) and approximately 4,279 across other states.
