Direct deposit

Have your dividend deposited straight into your account instead of waiting on a check in the mail.

Why shareholders use it

Dividends are paid by check by default. Direct deposit puts the money in your account on payment day — no waiting on the mail, no lost or stale-dated checks, and nothing to take to the bank.

The most recent dividend, approved by the Board on May 19th 2026, was $12.50 per share to shareholders of record on June 19th 2026.

Please do not email bank details. For your security, complete the authorization form and return it to the office by mail, or call (405) 235-5741 and we will walk you through it.

What you will need

Your shareholder account number, a voided check or a bank letter showing the routing and account numbers, and the signature of the registered holder.
