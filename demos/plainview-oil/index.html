Mineral Owner Intake

Tell us what you own.

Four quick questions. You do not need your deeds in front of you — “not sure” is a perfectly good answer, and we will do the title work.

What kind of interest do you own? Pick the closest — we will confirm it in the title work.
Where is it? County is enough to start. Section-Township-Range if you have it.
Is it producing, and what are you hoping to do? Two quick ones.
Where should we send it? A real person from Plainview will follow up — usually the same business day.

Submitting this does not obligate you to sell or lease anything, and it is not an offer. We will review what you own and follow up with next steps.

Professional Landwork Solutions

The title work behind every deal — for you, or for us.

We do this work for our own acquisitions, which is why we can do it for operators, investors, and families who need it done right.

Title & ownership

Mineral title review, chain of title verification, net mineral acre (NMA) calculations, and leasehold ownership verification.

Curative & probate

Heirship research, probate coordination, affidavit assistance, curative identification and resolution, and division order review.

Regulatory & development

Spacing and pooling review, multi-unit horizontal analysis, regulatory filing research, lease interpretation, and development risk review.

Valuations

Mineral and royalty evaluation built on production performance, decline curve analysis, development potential, and regulatory positioning.

Estates & families

Ownership clarification, probate coordination support, division order review, and asset liquidation structuring for inherited interests.

Surface coordination

Through affiliated relationships, coordinated strategies for surface property sales, ranch and acreage liquidation, and combined planning.
